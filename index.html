About Me

A Passion for Language & Learning

I am Shakhrizoda Rakhmonalieva, a EFL teacher and researcher from Uzbekistan. My work focuses on supporting secondary and higher education students. All I want is to connect academic theory and practical, real-world communication.

I’ve spent education and career exploring challenges and opportunities within Uzbekistan’s English language classrooms. I'm interested in how we can use digital tools and student-focused teaching to make learning English feel more natural and effective.

To me, teaching isn't just about grammar or vocabulary; it's about giving students the confidence to find their own voice and truly connect with the world. I’m also passionate about Central Asian perspectives in EFL teaching, our unique stories and insights in the global education community.
